More than a vending business. A value chain that includes people.

Kenya already has a strong flower industry. BloomBox opens a direct route to customers for the small growers and local florists who rarely reach premium retail.

Small-scale farmers get a market

  • Partner with small-scale growers, prioritising women-owned enterprises.
  • Buy through predictable supply agreements.
  • Open a direct-to-consumer market they can't easily reach today.
  • Train in post-harvest handling and quality standards.

The aim: more predictable income and better market access.

Women and youth become florists

  • Recruit unemployed young people and women.
  • Train them in bouquet arrangement.
  • Pay for the bouquets they prepare, by production or service contract.
  • Offer micro-franchises, and later preparation and replenishment hubs, to the strongest florists.

The aim: paid work and a path to running their own business.

Fewer flowers thrown away

  • Track stock freshness in every machine.
  • Forecast demand so we stock what sells.
  • Use recyclable packaging.
  • Partner with composting businesses for what can't be sold.

The aim: the smallest possible number of discarded flowers.

Flowers for social good

  • For every 10 bouquets sold, we fund one for someone in a community institution.
  • Customers can also buy a bouquet just to give away.
